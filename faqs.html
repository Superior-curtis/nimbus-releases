FAQs

Why does macOS say the app can’t be opened?

The beta is not notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch is blocked and a double-click simply fails. Right-click Nimbus in Applications and choose Open, then Open again in the dialog. Once only — every launch after that is normal. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Do I need a Mac with a notch?

No. Nimbus draws its own island at the top of the screen, so it works the same on a MacBook Air with a notch and on an external display without one. On notched Macs it sits around the notch rather than fighting it.

What does it cost?

Nothing while it is in beta. When it leaves beta it will be a one-time purchase — no subscription, no account, no upsell inside the app. Nothing in the beta expires.

Which macOS versions are supported?

Nimbus beta requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer. It works on Ventura, Sonoma and Sequoia. The current beta is built for Apple silicon Macs.

Apple silicon or Intel?

Apple silicon for now: M1, M2, M3, M4 and newer. Intel support would need a separate universal build.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. Windows is planned after the Mac beta is stable. The current download is a native macOS app and will not run on Windows.

Does Listen Together work across the internet?

In this beta, Listen Together is for friends on the same Wi-Fi. Add the friend in Settings with the address Nimbus shows on their Mac, then swipe up on the notch to host or join.

macOS says the developer can't be verified.

The beta is ad-hoc signed, which macOS treats as unidentified. Right-click the app and choose Open, then confirm — you only need to do this once.
